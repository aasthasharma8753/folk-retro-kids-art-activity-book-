# Master Resource Directory

External platforms for taking the Kalighat Reframe concept (`docs/ART_DIRECTION.md`) from spec to physical product. Referenced from `docs/PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md` at the relevant phase gates.

**Note:** these are third-party services, not vetted or contracted on this project's behalf — treat each as a starting point for your own due diligence (quotes, sample orders, certification scope) before committing budget, not as a pre-cleared vendor.

## 1. Layout & Digital Formatting Assets

- [Reedsy Studio](https://reedsy.com/studio/write-a-book/) — format early scroll-layout drafts, map page/panel pacing, free automated typesetting for early proofs.
- [Amazon KDP Publishing Hub](https://kdp.amazon.com/publish) — reference for children's book file dimensions and landscape-format print guidelines (useful for sanity-checking the 21:9 accordion-scroll trim before it goes to a specialty binder — KDP itself won't produce the Swiss-bound magnetic cover or deboss).

## 2. Global Materials Compliance & Safety Testing

Relevant to `PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md` Phase 0 (blocking gate — do not skip):

- [Intertek](https://intertek.com) — toy/children's-product testing lab; route the micro-encapsulated scent ink here for US child-safety testing (relevant standard to confirm scope on: **ASTM F963**, the US toy safety standard, alongside any ink-specific labeling requirement such as ASTM D-4236 for art materials — confirm with the lab which standard actually applies to an ingestible-risk scent-release ink for toddlers).
- [SGS](https://sgs.com) — quality inspection; audit the unbleached cotton pulp paper stock, run heavy-metal testing, and scope **EN71-3** (EU toy safety, migration of certain elements) compliance for the ink and paper together.

## 3. Custom Industrial Print & Manufacturing Sourcing

- [Alibaba Industrial Sourcing](https://alibaba.com) — sourcing marketplace for manufacturers producing heavy board books, embedded-magnet hardware, and accordion/leporello fold bindings. Vet any factory found here independently (samples, factory audit, reference checks) before a production commitment — Alibaba itself does not certify manufacturer quality.

**How this maps to the roadmap:** Phase 0's deboss vendor feasibility call and scent-ink safety certification scoping (`PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md`) are exactly where Intertek/SGS and an initial Alibaba supplier search get used first — before Phase 1's material prototypes are built.
