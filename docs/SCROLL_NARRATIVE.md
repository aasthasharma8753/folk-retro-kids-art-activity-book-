## Resolved: Panels 4 and 8 (formerly blocked)

The original draft proposed "Mowgli & Bagheera" for these panels. That was blocked (see `PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md` Phase 0 history) because Kipling's text is public domain but the *visual characters* as anyone recognizes them today are inseparable from Disney's actively-trademarked designs, and because *Jungle Book* is British colonial-era fiction, not Indian mythology — it never fit this roster's sourcing logic either.

**Pingalaka and Damanaka** (the lion king and his jackal minister) fix both problems: genuine Panchatantra characters, ancient Sanskrit public domain, on-roster with the rest of the Indian-folklore cast.

**One accuracy note before this goes further:** in their actual source tale (*Mitra-bheda*, "The Loss of Friends" — Panchatantra Book 1), Damanaka is not a helpful guide. He's a scheming minister who manipulates Pingalaka into distrusting and ultimately killing his best friend, a bull named Sanjivaka — it's a cautionary tale about gossip and betrayal, the opposite of the "teamwork/cooperation" framing in Panels 4 and 8 above. This product already takes similar liberties elsewhere (Panel 2 reframes Ghatotkach, a Mahabharata demon-warrior, as a gentle protector), so reimagining Pingalaka and Damanaka as a cooperative duo is a defensible creative choice for a toddler audience — **but it should be labeled as a reimagining, not presented as a faithful retelling**, the same way Panel 2's heritage_context already flags its own reframing ("through the lens of Bala... used for joyful protection"). Whoever does the cultural-consultant pass on this file (per the caveat above the table) should confirm this framing is comfortable being explicit about the departure from the source story, e.g. in any parent-facing "the story behind this page" copy.
