## Blocked: Panels 4 and 8

The original draft proposed **"Mowgli & Bagheera"** for Panel 4 (a jungle/vine scene) and a Ghatotkach + Mowgli collaborative-play scene for Panel 8. **Do not build these as drafted:**

- Rudyard Kipling's original *Jungle Book* text is public domain, but "Mowgli" and "Bagheera" as anyone would actually draw or recognize them today are inseparable from Disney's actively-trademarked 1967 film character designs (and multiple later Disney productions). This is a real infringement/trade-dress exposure, not a stylistic-homage gray area like the Doordarshan-serial caution already on the roster (`ART_DIRECTION.md` §2) — it's a different, better-known, more litigious rights holder.
- It also breaks the roster's own sourcing logic: every other character in this product comes from Indian epic/folklore (Mahabharata, Ramayana, South Indian oral tradition). Kipling's *Jungle Book*, despite its India setting, is a British colonial-era literary work, not Indian mythology — it doesn't actually belong in this roster on a cultural-sourcing basis either, independent of the IP risk.

**Recommended replacement:** a **Panchatantra** fable (ancient Sanskrit animal-fable collection, unambiguously public domain, zero modern IP overhang) fits the "wild/jungle/nature" panel role Panel 4 was reaching for — e.g., a jackal, lion, or monkey figure from a classic Panchatantra tale — and keeps the roster's Indian-folklore sourcing consistent. This needs a specific tale/character pick before Panel 4 (and the Ghatotkach pairing in Panel 8) can be finalized — that's a creative decision for you to make, not one to default silently.
